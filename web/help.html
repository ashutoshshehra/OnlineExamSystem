Everything you need to know about taking tests, proctoring rules, and teacher tools.

Anti-Cheating Rules

Examinations mandate full-screen mode. Switching tabs or minimizing the window registers a strike. If 3 strikes occur, the exam is automatically submitted immediately. Copy-pasting, inspecting (F12), and right-clicking are strictly locked.

AI Question Generator

Teachers can launch the AI Exam Builder, enter any curriculum topic, select question count and difficulty, and the AI will synthesize questions, choices, correct answers, and educational explanations in seconds.

Verifiable Certificates

Upon completing an assessment with a passing grade (40%+), students can generate a high-resolution printable Certificate of Achievement equipped with a dynamic QR verification code.

Supported Academic Grades

The curriculum includes pre-built tests across 9 levels: Class 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, and College / CS. Students can filter assessments to match their specific grade.
